About Us

Get Your Flame On

Grill Out started with one simple idea: food tastes better over real fire. What began as a small grill counter on GT Road, Haripur has grown into a favorite neighborhood spot for burgers, steaks, and BBQ platters — every order fired up fresh, never pre-made, never rushed.

From our string-lit outdoor seating to our sizzling steak platters, everything we serve is built around one promise: fresh, tasty, and healthy — with a whole lot of flame.
